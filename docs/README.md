# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## Overview

This directory contains the comprehensive project management processes and guides used by OctoAcme teams to deliver products and features consistently and effectively. Whether you're starting a new project, planning work, managing execution, or capturing learnings, these documents provide the frameworks, checklists, and templates to ensure success.

## OctoAcme Project Management Approach

OctoAcme follows a customer-first, iterative delivery model with clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and psychological safety. Our projects move through five key phases:

1. **Initiation** – Validate problem statement, identify stakeholders, define high-level goals
2. **Planning** – Break work into shippable increments, estimate scope, manage dependencies
3. **Execution** – Build, test, review, and iterate with daily standups and regular demos
4. **Release** – Deploy to production with confidence through checklists and rollback plans
5. **Close & Retrospective** – Capture learnings and drive continuous improvement

## Process Summary

OctoAcme's project management framework emphasizes structured yet flexible delivery. Every project begins with a lightweight One-pager that validates business need and confirms stakeholder alignment—a critical decision gate before detailed planning proceeds. Projects progress through well-defined phases with clear role separation: **Project Managers** coordinate schedules and communications, **Product Managers** define outcomes and prioritize work, **Developers** implement features with quality and testability, and **QA teams** validate acceptance criteria.

The organization maintains consistent communication through weekly PM-PdM alignment syncs, twice-weekly team standups, and monthly stakeholder updates. Quality and risk management are embedded throughout: small pull requests (≤400 lines), automated CI/CD testing, mandatory code reviews, and comprehensive pre-release checklists ensure reliable delivery. A Risk Register updated weekly and an incident playbook provide visibility and mitigation strategies. Post-sprint retrospectives capture learnings and drive continuous improvement, creating a culture where small, iterative changes compound into organizational excellence.

## Key Principles

- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability in all decisions
- **Iterative delivery**: Ship small, testable increments and learn from feedback
- **Clear ownership**: Assign named Project Manager and Product Lead for each project
- **Data-informed**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence and metrics
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback, learning, and blameless retrospectives
